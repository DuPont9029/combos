# Snake-Eye Ash 1-card and 2-card combos

**Deck:** #snake-eye-fire-king · **Video:** [3 MUST KNOW SNAKE EYES COMBOS! Snake Eyes Guide!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SXYGZkozoMU) · **Channel:** Moha - Master Duel

> [!abstract] Summary
> The video shows three lines: the best 2-card combo (**Snake-Eye Ash** + **Black Witch**), a 1-card OTK that destroys 5 cards with only **Snake-Eye Ash**, and the best 1-card combo with only **Snake-Eye Ash**. The lines play through **Snake-Eyes Poplar**, **Snake-Eye Oak**, **Fire King High Avatar Garunix**-style Flame Berga recursion (via **Flame Berga**), and **Divine Temple of the Snake-Eye**. The 2-card combo ends on a board of **Accesscode Talker**-style disruption: 7 disruptions plus Appalissa protection. The 1-card lines end on a board with 6 disruptions plus **Linkuriboh**.

> [!tip]- Key concepts
> - **Snake-Eye Ash** is the only starter needed: it searches **Snake-Eyes Poplar**, which self-summons when searched.
> - **Snake-Eyes Poplar** is a Link material that goes back face up to the Spell & Trap Zone when sent to the GY.
> - **Snake-Eye Oak** and **Snake-Eye Ash** trade places via Oak's summon effect, recycling Ash to get more value.
> - **Flame Berga** is summoned from the deck by Oak and, when sent to the GY, summons two Level 1 monsters.
> - **Jet Synchron** can be revived from the GY by discarding 1 card, giving repeatable Synchro/Link material.
> - **Sprite Elf** protects your key monsters from targeting and revives **Formula Synchron**; summon your most important monsters in its zones.
> - On the opponent's turn, **Formula Synchron** and **Flame Berga** start a chain that sets up a strong second end board.
> - Chain order on the opponent's turn matters; the video stresses the exact chain link order.
> - **Linkuriboh** protects **Apalpapillon**-style Link 4 via tribute and returns on the opponent's turn.
> - The OTK line wins with over 8k damage using **Zalantis**-style mass banish and re-summon.

## Starting hand

**Line 1: best 2-card combo**
- **Snake-Eye Ash**
- **Black Witch** (or any way to search it)

**Line 2: 1-card OTK (destroy 5)**
- **Snake-Eye Ash**

**Line 3: best 1-card combo**
- **Snake-Eye Ash**

## Combo

### Line 1: best 2-card combo (Ash + Black Witch)

1. **Normal Summon** **Snake-Eye Ash**.
2. **Activate** **Snake-Eye Ash**: search **Snake-Eyes Poplar**.
3. **Activate** **Snake-Eyes Poplar** (when added to hand): Special Summon itself.
4. **Activate** **Snake-Eyes Poplar** (on summon): search **Divine Temple of the Snake-Eye**.
5. **Link Summon** **Linkuriboh** using **Snake-Eyes Poplar**.
6. **Activate** **Snake-Eyes Poplar** (sent to the GY): place itself face up in the Spell & Trap Zone.
7. **Activate** **Wanted** (the Spell/Trap from Poplar's zone effect): search **Black Witch**.
8. **Special Summon** **Black Witch** (alternatively, if you have it, summon it directly) by sending **Snake-Eyes Poplar** from the field to the GY.
9. **Activate** **Black Witch** (on summon): set the original Spell/Trap.
10. **Activate** the **Original** (Spell/Trap): send **Linkuriboh** from the field to the GY to Special Summon **Jet Synchron**.
11. **Link Summon** **Savage Dragon** using **Jet Synchron** and **Black Witch**.
12. **Activate** **Savage Dragon**: equip **Linkuriboh** to it to gain an Omni Negate.
13. **Activate** **Divine Temple of the Snake-Eye**: place **Flame Berga** face up in the Spell & Trap Zone.
14. **Activate** **Snake-Eye Ash**: send itself and **Linkuriboh** to the GY to Special Summon **Snake-Eye Oak** from the deck.
15. **Activate** **Snake-Eye Oak** (on summon): Special Summon **Jet Synchron** from the GY.
16. **Activate** **Snake-Eye Oak**: send itself and **Flame Berga** (from the Spell & Trap Zone) to the GY to Special Summon **Flame Berga** from the deck.
17. **Activate** **Flame Berga** (sent to the GY): Special Summon two Level 1 monsters.
18. **Synchro Summon** **Formula Synchron** using **Jet Synchron** and any Level 1 monster.
19. **Activate** **Jet Synchron**: draw 1.
20. **Activate** **Jet Synchron** (in the GY): discard 1 card to Special Summon itself.
21. **Link Summon** **Mascarina** using **Jet Synchron** and the Level 1 monster.
22. **Link Summon** **Promethean Princess** using **Mascarina** and **Flame Berga**.
23. **Activate** **Promethean Princess**: Special Summon **Flame Berga** from the GY.
24. **Activate** **Flame Berga**: place **Mascarina** face up in the Spell & Trap Zone.
25. **Link Summon** **Sprite Elf** using **Formula Synchron** and **Promethean Princess**.
26. **Activate** **Sprite Elf**: Special Summon **Formula Synchron** back. Summon your most important monsters in the zones where **Sprite Elf** protects from targeting.
27. **Activate** **Wanted** (in the GY): draw 1.
28. **End Phase**: end your turn.

**Opponent's turn**

29. **Activate** **Formula Synchron**.
30. **Activate** **Flame Berga** (in chain): Special Summon **Mascarina**.
31. **Synchro Summon** **Baronne de Fleur**-style Synchro (**Baran**) using **Formula Synchron** and **Flame Berga**.
32. **Activate** **Flame Berga** (sent to the GY as material): Special Summon **Snake-Eye Ash** and **Snake-Eyes Poplar**.
33. Build the chain in this exact order:
    - Chain Link 1: **Snake-Eye Ash**
    - Chain Link 2: **Snake-Eyes Poplar**
    - Chain Link 3: **Linkuriboh** (tribute any one of them)
    - Chain Link 4: **Mascarina**
    - Chain Link 5: **Sprite Elf**
34. Resolve the chain: **Sprite Elf** Special Summons **Formula Synchron**.
35. **Link Summon** **Apalpapillon**-style Link 4 (**Appalissa**) using **Mascarina** and **Snake-Eye Ash** (then **Formula Synchron**, **Mascarina** and **Sprite Elf** as the 4 materials).
36. **Special Summon** **Linkuriboh** (summons itself).
37. **Activate** **Snake-Eye Ash**: search for follow-up.
38. **Activate** **Snake-Eyes Poplar**: place **Flame Berga** face up in the Spell & Trap Zone, which the field spell can summon.

### Line 2: 1-card OTK (destroy 5, Ash only)

1. **Normal Summon** **Snake-Eye Ash**.
2. **Activate** **Snake-Eye Ash**: search **Snake-Eyes Poplar**.
3. **Activate** **Snake-Eyes Poplar** (when searched): Special Summon itself.
4. **Activate** **Snake-Eyes Poplar** (on summon): search **Divine Temple of the Snake-Eye**.
5. **Link Summon** **Linkuriboh** using **Snake-Eyes Poplar**.
6. **Activate** **Snake-Eyes Poplar**: place itself face up in the Spell & Trap Zone.
7. **Activate** **Snake-Eye Ash**: send itself and **Snake-Eyes Poplar** to the GY to Special Summon **Snake-Eye Oak** from the deck.
8. **Activate** **Snake-Eye Oak**: Special Summon **Snake-Eye Ash** from the GY.
9. **Activate** **Divine Temple of the Snake-Eye**: place **Flame Berga** face up in the Spell & Trap Zone.
10. **Activate** **Snake-Eye Oak**: send itself and **Flame Berga** to the GY to Special Summon **Flame Berga** from the deck.
11. **Activate** **Flame Berga** (sent to the GY): Special Summon any two Level 1 FIRE monsters from the GY.
12. **Activate** **Flame Berga**: get rid of one of your opponent's monsters.
13. **Link Summon** any FIRE Link 2 using any Level 1 monsters.
14. **Link Summon** **Promethean Princess** using the Link 2 and the Link 1.
15. **Activate** **Promethean Princess**: bring back your FIRE Link 2.
16. **Link Summon** **Ammo Whale** (**Amblow Whale**) using **Promethean Princess** and any Level 1 monster.
17. **Link Summon** **Zalantis** using **Amblow Whale**.
18. **Activate** **Zalantis**: banish all monsters on the field and re-summon them. Summon **Zalantis** in the middle; the rest can be placed anywhere.
19. **Activate** **Promethean Princess** (in the GY, triggered because monsters were summoned to the opponent's field): destroy the FIRE Link 2 and an opponent's monster to Special Summon itself next to **Zalantis**. This is the second monster pop.
20. **Activate** **Amblow Whale**: because a Link 3 or lower was destroyed, destroy another card (third destruction).
21. **Battle Phase**: **Zalantis** destroys two more cards (total of five destructions) for over 8k lethal damage.

### Line 3: best 1-card combo (Ash only)

1. **Normal Summon** **Snake-Eye Ash**.
2. **Activate** **Snake-Eye Ash**: search **Snake-Eyes Poplar**.
3. **Activate** **Snake-Eyes Poplar**: Special Summon itself.
4. **Activate** **Snake-Eyes Poplar** (on summon): search the **Original** (Spell/Trap).
5. **Link Summon** **Linkuriboh** using **Snake-Eyes Poplar**.
6. **Activate** **Snake-Eyes Poplar** (sent to the GY): place itself in the Spell & Trap Zone.
7. **Activate** the **Original**: send **Snake-Eyes Poplar** to the GY to Special Summon **Jet Synchron**.
8. **Activate** **Snake-Eye Ash**: send itself and **Linkuriboh** to the GY to Special Summon **Snake-Eye Oak**.
9. **Activate** **Snake-Eye Oak** (on summon): Special Summon **Snake-Eye Ash**.
10. **Activate** **Snake-Eye Oak**: send itself and the card it summoned to the GY to Special Summon **Flame Berga** from the deck.
11. **Link Summon** **Mascarina** using **Flame Berga** and **Jet Synchron**.
12. **Activate** **Flame Berga** (sent to the GY): Special Summon **Jet Synchron** and any other Level 1 monster.
13. **Synchro Summon** **Formula Synchron** using those two monsters.
14. **Activate** **Jet Synchron** (in the GY): discard any card to Special Summon itself.
15. **Link Summon** **Promethean Princess** using **Jet Synchron** and **Mascarina**.
16. **Activate** **Promethean Princess**: Special Summon **Flame Berga**.
17. **Link Summon** **Sprite Elf** using **Promethean Princess** and **Formula Synchron**.
18. **Activate** **Sprite Elf**: bring back **Formula Synchron**.
19. **Activate** **Flame Berga**: place **Mascarina** face up in the Spell & Trap Zone.
20. **End Phase**: end your turn.

**Opponent's turn**

21. **Activate** **Formula Synchron**.
22. **Activate** **Flame Berga** (in chain): Special Summon **Mascarina**.
23. **Synchro Summon** **Baronne de Fleur**-style Synchro (**Baran**) using **Formula Synchron** (and **Flame Berga**), making use of **Sprite Elf**'s protection.
24. **Activate** **Flame Berga** (sent to the GY): Special Summon **Snake-Eye Oak** and **Snake-Eye Ash**.
25. **Activate** **Snake-Eye Oak**: Special Summon **Snake-Eyes Poplar**.
26. **Activate** **Snake-Eye Ash**: search for follow-up.
27. Build the chain in this exact order:
    - Chain Link 1: **Linkuriboh**
    - Chain Link 2: **Mascarina**
    - Chain Link 3: **Sprite Elf**
28. Resolve the chain: **Sprite Elf** Special Summons **Formula Synchron**.
29. **Link Summon** **Apalpapillon**-style Link 4 (**Appalissa**) using the two Level 1 monsters, **Formula Synchron** and **Mascarina**, under **Sprite Elf**'s protection.
30. **Special Summon** **Linkuriboh** (summons itself).
31. **Activate** **Snake-Eyes Poplar**: place **Flame Berga** face up in the Spell & Trap Zone.

## End board

**Line 1: 2-card combo (7 disruptions plus Appalissa protection)**
- **Appalissa**: 4 monster negates.
- **Baran**: Omni Negate.
- **Savage Dragon**: Omni Negate (with equipped **Linkuriboh**).
- **Promethean Princess**: destroys a monster.
- **Linkuriboh**: protects **Appalissa**.
- **Flame Berga** face up in the Spell & Trap Zone for follow-up, summoned by the field spell.
- Search from **Snake-Eye Ash** for follow-up.

**Line 2: 1-card OTK**
- **Zalantis** (middle Extra Monster Zone area), **Promethean Princess**, **Amblow Whale** and re-summoned monsters.
- Five destructions in total (Flame Berga effect, Promethean Princess, Amblow Whale, two from **Zalantis** in the Battle Phase).
- Over 8k lethal damage.

**Line 3: best 1-card combo (6 disruptions plus Linkuriboh)**
- **Appalissa**: 4 monster negates.
- **Baran**: Omni Negate.
- **Promethean Princess**: monster pop.
- **Linkuriboh**: protects **Appalissa**.
- **Flame Berga** face up in the Spell & Trap Zone.

## Notes

> [!important] Chain order on the opponent's turn
> The order of the chain is stressed in both lines:
> - Line 1: Chain Link 1 **Snake-Eye Ash**, 2 **Snake-Eyes Poplar**, 3 **Linkuriboh** (tribute any one of them), 4 **Mascarina**, 5 **Sprite Elf**.
> - Line 3: Chain Link 1 **Linkuriboh**, 2 **Mascarina**, 3 **Sprite Elf**.

> [!warning] Protect your key monsters
> Summon your most important monsters in the zones where **Sprite Elf** protects them from targeting, both on your turn and when making **Appalissa** on the opponent's turn.

> [!example] Alternative line
> In the 2-card combo, if you already have **Black Witch** in hand, you can skip searching it with **Wanted** and just Special Summon **Black Witch** by sending **Snake-Eyes Poplar** from the field to the GY.

- In the 2-card combo you can also activate **Wanted** in the GY to draw 1 before ending your turn.
- The OTK line requires only **Snake-Eye Ash**; **Zalantis** must be summoned in the middle so **Promethean Princess** can be summoned next to it.
